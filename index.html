About Us

Meet the Duo

The faces — and hands — behind every pour

Carissa Hefta

Co-Founder & Lead Mixologist

United States Bartenders' Guild — Member #49727

Carissa Hefta has been behind a bar for over 20 years — and not just pouring drinks. She managed Parliament Pub in Omaha for seven years as Lead Bartender and Manager: scheduling, inventory, ordering, staff training, and running a full operation under pressure night after night. Before that, she worked a specialty martini bar with a 100-cocktail menu she had memorized cold. She didn't read from a list. She knew it. She's bartended Gasparilla, College World Series events, and New Year's Eve countdowns — large crowds, outdoor rigs, high-ticket nights. A USBG Bar Professional and certified mixologist, Carissa founded Heftology because she already knew how to run someone else's bar. Now she runs her own.

Michelle Flannery

Co-Founder & Lead Bartender

United States Bartenders' Guild — Member #49774

Michelle Flannery ran the prime bar at Circles Waterfront Restaurant for six years — not a satellite station, not a service bar. The main stage. Blenders, fresh squeezers, deep freezers, and one of Tampa Bay's most demanding cocktail menus, executed flawlessly under full service pressure night after night. She didn't just tend bar. She commanded it. The kind of head bartender that venues build their drink program around and regulars come back specifically to see. She brings that same precision, creativity, and unshakeable calm to every Heftology event — the difference is now she comes to you. Heftology just gave her a home base.

Together, they've got your event covered — from the first consultation to the last call. 🍸

Questions

Good to Know

How do I book my date?

A 50% deposit holds your date. We send an invoice once your event details are confirmed, and your date is held when the deposit is paid. You also sign our agreement and authorize a card on file for the balance.

When is the balance due?

The remaining balance is due 5 days before your event and is charged to your card on file. We send a reminder beforehand. If the balance isn't paid, we can't hold your date, and the deposit is kept.

Is the deposit refundable?

No. The deposit holds your date and covers the planning we start right away. If you cancel early, it becomes credit toward a future booking, as described below.

What if I need to cancel?

Tell us as soon as you know. The deposit is non-refundable, but if you cancel at least 30 days before your event, we apply it as credit toward a future Heftology booking within 12 months. Closer than that, we keep the deposit. Once the balance is charged, 5 days before your event, it is non-refundable.

Can I reschedule?

We can move your event to a new date once, subject to our availability, if you ask at least 14 days before the original date.

What if my guest count changes?

Let us know at least 7 days before your event so we can staff and prepare.

What if Carissa or Michelle can't make it?

Your event doesn't depend on just one of us. If Carissa or Michelle can't make it, a beautiful and talented bartender we know and trust steps in, and the party doesn't skip a beat.

Do you provide the alcohol?

No. You bring the spirits, and we bring the craft. We can help you plan what to buy.
